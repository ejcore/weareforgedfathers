Training

Training Expectations

3 full body workouts each week. Your sessions are delivered in TrainHeroic.

1The expectation

The expectation is that you're able to complete the 3 full body workouts each week.

If you can't get all 3 in, that's ok. Aim to go for a walk, do some push ups, sit ups, squats, anything you can do on the day you can't make it to the gym.

Time

These workouts should hopefully only take 45 minutes. If you have longer, take your time. If you need to speed it up, get what you can in 30 minutes.

Your schedule

Feel free to move your training sessions to the days that work best for you. Work, kids and life come first. Fit the sessions around them.

2Allow rest

Your motivation will be high starting this program. Don't overdo it. It's not about how hard you can go for a short period of time, it's about the consistency you can apply for weeks and months on end.

3Missed the gym? Do this instead

Walk

Get outside. Take the kids or the dog.

Push ups

Sets through the day. Every rep counts.

Sit ups

A few sets before bed or in the morning.

Squats

Bodyweight. In the living room is fine.

4Using TrainHeroic

  • Your 3 full body sessions are in the app each week. Open the session for the day and work through it in order.
  • Log your weights and reps. That's how we see progression and know when to add weight.
  • Supersets show as 1A-1B, 2A-2B. Tempo and RIR are written on each exercise. Page 05 explains both.
  • If your gym doesn't have a machine, sub it out. More on that on page 05.

Training

Understanding the Training Principles

What you'll see in your TrainHeroic sessions, and how to use it.

1Reps in reserve (RIR)

This is the number of reps before you reach muscular or technical failure. 3 RIR = 3 reps in the tank, or 3 reps short of failure.

This takes time to learn and figure out, so don't stress this too much.

RIR quick read

3 RIR3 more good reps left
2 RIRHard, but 2 left
1 RIROne rep left with good form
0 RIRFailure. Nothing left

2Tempo

In the program you'll see something like 1c, x, 3e, x tempo.

C
Concentric

When you exert force. The push or the pull.

E
Eccentric

The stretch. When the weight is returning to starting position.

X
No pause

No pause between the concentric and the eccentric.

Ideally just use a controlled tempo and don't feel you have to count every rep.

3Supersets

These are labelled 1A-1B, 2A-2B and so on. Do the exercises back to back and then take a rest.

The main purpose of a superset is to save time. If you want or have to split them up, you can.

5Substitutions

Your gym won't necessarily have the same equipment my gym does, so sub machines out as you need to. A chest press is a chest press. If the machine is similar and you like it, use it. Doing movements in a different order is also fine, as you need to be efficient with your time.

4Weight progression

Only increase weight if you have hit the top end of the rep range. Example: 8-12 reps and you hit 12 by week 2 or 3.

Don't increase weight if you feel you can't maintain form or control of the exercise. Remember the season of life you're in, especially if you haven't worked out in a while. You don't want an injury because you went too hard too fast. Allow the body to adapt and build up steadily.

Training

Recovery

Training is where you break down. Recovery is where you build back stronger.

7-8 hrs
Sleep

There is no better recovery tool than sleep. Poor sleep will impact cognitive function, muscle recovery, your hormones and more. Ideally 7-8 hours per night, but in this season that can be difficult. Just do your best to get what you can.

1 gal
Hydration

This is also key. Aim for a gallon of water per day. If you're hungry, sometimes you're just dehydrated. Proper hydration will also aid digestion and keep you regular.

Rest
Fatigue management

There is a difference between being lazy and being run down or sick. If you feel sick or completely under recovered, take an extra rest day between training. Running yourself further into the ground will not help progression.

Nutrition

Nutrition Mission

Nutrition is where the magic happens.

I want you to look at training as the avenue for building strength, getting fit, improving mobility and so on. But nutrition is where the magic happens when it comes to body composition goals.

If you don't commit to consistent application of the nutrition protocols, you won't improve to the level you're capable of.

1Consistency

You don't have to be perfect with your nutrition. You're in a busy season of life and sometimes you have to do what you can. I will give you fast and easy on the go options you can use when needed (page 12).

Don't guilt yourself if you have a slip. Get right back on track as if nothing happened.

Will Carter

2Fueling performance

Even if your goal is fat loss, the goal is not to create the largest deficit possible and be excessively restrictive. The goal is to eat high volume foods that allow you to feel satiated and fuel performance. Once you do this, you'll notice such a huge difference in how you feel.

Oats

Quick oats in the morning.

Rice

Jasmine or basmati.

Potatoes

Sweet or white.

Fruit + sourdough

Great carbs to build in.

3Meal examples, not a meal template

I will give meal examples but not a meal template, as I want you to eat according to your lifestyle and circumstances. A meal template is strict and will not allow you to make your own decisions as you go.

Eating for health is a lifestyle, not a short term fix.

Nutrition

Tracking, Fasting and 90/10

Tools you can use when you're ready. None of them have to be perfect to work.

1Tracking macros

If you feel comfortable tracking macros, reach out to me and I will give you macro numbers to follow. I do advocate for learning to do this and doing it for a period of time, so you gain better awareness of what you're putting into your body and the serving sizes you need.

Once you have done it for a while, you gain good awareness to then eyeball your serving sizes and know that you're close enough. If you want to track macros for a much longer period of time, you absolutely can. Just know you don't have to be absolutely perfect for it to work.

The app I recommend: MyFitnessPal. Huge food database, barcode scanner, and most restaurant chains are already in it. Download it here: [WILL'S MYFITNESSPAL REFERRAL LINK]

2Intermittent fasting

Some of you might like to use this as a way to limit how much food you can consume. I know some of you like to eat big meals, so intermittent fasting lets you eat larger meals because your eating window is smaller.

You can have an 8 hour window to eat that suits you, whether that's getting food in earlier in the day or later in the afternoon and evening. This doesn't allow you to eat whatever you want. The goal to eat quality nutrition is still there, but it might help you have better compliance to the plan.

3Getting started in MyFitnessPal

1. Download

Use the link above and set up your profile.

2. Get your numbers

Message me and I'll give you macro numbers to enter in the app.

3. Log honestly

Scan barcodes, weigh what you can, eyeball the rest. Close enough works.

Nutrition

No Cheat Meals. 90/10.

Enjoy date night and ice cream with your kids, without letting it derail you.

1Why I don't use the term

I personally dislike this term, because you don't cheat on your diet. You aren't in a relationship with food. Eating is something we have to do to fuel our bodies, recover and perform better.

I'm a huge advocate for enjoying date nights with your significant other and ice cream with your kids, and I don't want you to have to sacrifice all of that. What I want for you is that you're able to do those things without letting it derail you completely.

90/10

If you want faster, better progression. 90% of your diet is healthy, nutritious foods that support your goals. 10% is for the foods you love, with a level of control.

80/20

If you feel you have more freedom and enjoyment for life this way, that works. 80% nutritious foods, 20% for enjoying the foods you love.

The goal is not for a pizza night to turn into a pizza, ice cream and candy night, but to make the decision on what you really want. Perhaps you don't need the entire pizza. Have 2 slices, then eat protein and vegetables on the side to fill you up and help you stay on track.

2Make the decision

Pizza night

2 slices, then protein and vegetables on the side to fill you up.

Date night

Pick the one thing you really want, the dessert or the drink. Protein and veggies with the meal.

Ice cream with the kids

Have it. Be there. Next meal, back on track as if nothing happened.

Birthday parties

Eat protein before you go so you aren't starving. Enjoy a slice of cake.
