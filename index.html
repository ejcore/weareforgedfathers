Nutrition is where the magic happens.

Training builds strength, fitness and mobility. Nutrition is what changes your body composition. You don't have to be perfect. You have to be consistent.

Start here

Simple targets

No calorie counting on day one. Hit these and build from there.

  • 1 gallon of water per day
  • 1 to 2 palms of protein per meal
  • 1 to 2 fists of veggies and/or fruit
  • 1 cup of carbs (potatoes, rice, oats)
  • 1 thumb of healthy fats (olive oil, nuts, avocado)
When you're ready

Macros from Will

Feel comfortable tracking? Reach out and Will gives you macro numbers to follow. Track for a while, learn your serving sizes, then eyeball it and know you're close enough.

  • Meal examples, not a strict meal template
  • Will recommends MyFitnessPal for tracking
  • Optional 8 hour eating window if fasting helps you stay on plan
90/10or 80/20

You don't cheat on your diet.

Enjoy date night with your wife and ice cream with your kids. 80 to 90% of what you eat supports your goals. The rest is for the foods you love, with control. Pizza night doesn't have to become pizza, ice cream and candy night. Slip up? Get right back on track as if nothing happened.

The full member starter pack, with meal examples and Will's grocery list, comes when you join. Not medical advice. Check with your doctor before big diet changes.
